## RAVDESS

**Dataset:** The Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song (RAVDESS)

**Creators:** Steven R. Livingstone and Frank A. Russo

**Official source:**  
https://zenodo.org/records/1188976

**DOI:**  
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1188976

**Publication:**  
Livingstone, S. R., & Russo, F. A. (2018). The Ryerson Audio-Visual Database
of Emotional Speech and Song (RAVDESS): A dynamic, multimodal set of facial
and vocal expressions in North American English. *PLOS ONE, 13*(5), e0196391.  
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0196391

**License:**  
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)

**Use in this project:**  
RAVDESS is used as an emotion-speech dataset for development and evaluation
of the affect classification component.
